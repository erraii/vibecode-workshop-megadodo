# The idea

- Assigned idea (confirmed): Secret Santa — randomly assign gift partners and let participants add wishlists within a shared budget.
- Who has the problem? Agreed: an organiser arranging a small gift exchange and participants who need to know whom to buy for and what they would like.
- Demo outcome (agreed): an organiser creates an exchange with a spending limit, participants join and add wishlists, the organiser draws once, and each participant privately sees their recipient and wishlist.
- Proof task (agreed): four teammates join from separate browser sessions; after the draw each sees one other recipient, every participant receives one gift, and the results remain the same after reload.
- Constraints: preserve workshop deployment contracts. Confirmed: private links without accounts; organiser has no access to all matches; budget is a stated per-gift limit in euros (EUR), without price verification. Team: Eray, Monir, Aysegul and Atousa. Time constraints are unspecified.
- Agreed exclusions: accounts, email delivery, purchases, price verification, exclusion rules, redraws and late joins after the draw. Agreed as part of M1 on 2026-10-20.
- Agreed lifecycle: membership closes when the organiser draws; wishlists can be edited after the draw. Private links grant access to their holder; no account recovery is proposed for M1. Organiser access must not reveal the full assignment list through UI or API.
- M1 approved on 2026-10-20; accountable human owner: Eray. Deployment remains subject to an explicit milestone release instruction.
